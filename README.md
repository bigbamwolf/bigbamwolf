### Arwin Bagaslao

I run a small group of businesses in Manila and build the software that runs them. An ad production house, a fragrance retail operation across four sales channels, a food cart, and a trading desk. Since May 1, 2026 almost all of it runs on Claude Code: 49 custom agents, a pile of hooks, and launchd jobs that keep working while I sleep.

Most of what I build is internal and stays that way. What lands here is the infrastructure underneath it, the parts that are useful to anyone.

**Start here**
* [claude-code-empire](https://github.com/bigbamwolf/claude-code-empire): how one person runs seven businesses on Claude Code. Agent pack, hooks, the self improving loop, and the lessons that cost me the most.

**The toolbox**
* [phoenix](https://github.com/bigbamwolf/phoenix): persistent task runner. Carries a long task to completion across usage limits, context resets, and a closed laptop.
* [cerberus](https://github.com/bigbamwolf/cerberus): browser automation escalation ladder. Reads the failure and climbs to the head that can beat it.
* [relay](https://github.com/bigbamwolf/relay): a usage cap drops a model tier instead of stopping the task.
* [launchd-survival-kit](https://github.com/bigbamwolf/launchd-survival-kit): keep dozens of always on jobs alive on an 8GB Mac without kernel panics.
* [empirehud](https://github.com/bigbamwolf/empirehud): a live HUD mod for Claude Code.

**How I work**
Deterministic Python over prompts wherever a prompt is not required. Ledgers on disk over state in a context window. Every automation that runs unattended has a hard gate in front of money, deletion, and anything that leaves the building.

Manila, Philippines.
